How verification works

  1. Enter an email address you own and create a password.
  2. Open your inbox and follow the Supabase verification email. If you don't receive it, check Spam or Promotions.
  3. Return here and sign in. On Android, return to the app after confirming in your email/browser.
  4. Residents can explore updates, log demonstration reports and see report IDs in Activity.

Account permissions: A verified public account is a resident account. It does not unlock the restricted operator dashboard or the independent simulated reviewer workspace.

This hackathon prototype uses fictional incidents. It is not connected to municipal incident feeds or certified water quality data.
