THE GABA WORKFLOW

From a concern to a clear response.

A traceable journey, demonstrated with fictional data. Municipal verification requires formal operator integration and is not yet available in the live public demo.

01Report

A resident records a leak, service interruption or water concern; offline reports are queued on the device.

02Review

Authorised staff assess the report with available service and infrastructure evidence.

03Respond

Human operators route inspections, record decisions and update incident states.

04Verify

Evidence supports closure, while public updates distinguish reports from confirmed information.

Transparent by design. This submission is a working prototype and uses synthetic water-infrastructure data. It is not a live municipal advisory, quality certification or public emergency reporting channel.

PRACTICAL WATER GUIDE

Be ready.
Use water wisely.

Simple actions for daily life, even without a connected water meter. These are general guidelines, not water-safety advisories.

01 / PREPARE

Before a water interruption

Follow notices from your water provider. Plan essential drinking, hygiene and household needs, store water safely, and check whether your facility has an emergency plan.

02 / RESPOND

When a pipe bursts

Keep away from flowing water around electricity or unstable ground. Report the location and visible problem to the authorised service provider. Do not attempt repairs to public infrastructure.

03 / SAVE

Estimate shower water use

Enter a shower duration and estimated flow to understand how much water that activity could use.

63 LEstimated water used per shower. Actual usage depends on showerhead flow and pressure.
04 / WATER QUALITY

Check the official source

Water pressure and flow cannot prove drinking water is safe. Follow verified advisories and health guidance from your responsible water provider.
